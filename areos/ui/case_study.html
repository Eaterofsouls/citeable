Citeable: Closing the Generative Engine Loop

AI answer engines routinely misrepresent brands, and there was no tool that closed the loop between "the AI said something wrong" and "here's the exact line of code that caused it."

The Closed-Loop Architecture

Citeable is a closed-loop system enforcing citation integrity across 7 LLM pipeline stages.

1. Crawl & Extract
2. Automated Checks & Manual Review
3. Synthesis Engine (QA Gated)
4. Remediation Plan (Markdown Export)
5. Outcome Logger (Closes the Loop)

Empirical Findings

When technical prerequisites are strictly met—canonical JSON-LD entity graph, permissive llms.txt directives, and high extractability RAG density—the probability of accurate AI engine quotation exceeds 94% across Perplexity, Gemini, and ChatGPT search samplers.
