# C082 — Digital-PR / Third-Party Citation Gap

## What AREOS Does Automatically
- Records which domains were cited alongside or instead of the target brand.
- Tracks share-of-voice (how often brand is cited vs. competitors in the same prompt set).

## What You Must Do Manually

### Step 1 — Identify which third parties are winning the citation
From the citation sampling report, list every domain that was cited when the brand was not.

For each: What type of site is it?
- Industry publication / trade media
- Competitor's own site
- Aggregator / review platform (G2, Trustpilot, Capterra)
- Wikipedia / general knowledge source
- Analyst firm (Gartner, Forrester)

### Step 2 — Understand why they're winning
Open each winning third-party page and compare it to the brand's page for the same topic.

Ask: *"What does this third-party page have that the brand's page doesn't?"*

Common patterns:
- The third-party page uses simpler, more direct language.
- It has a neutral, educational tone (LLMs prefer neutral sources).
- It's older / more frequently linked-to.
- It explicitly compares multiple brands (LLMs trust comparative content).
- It has schema markup the brand's page lacks.

### Step 3 — Build the PR gap map
Create a table:

| Query | Who's cited | Why they're winning | Brand gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| "What is [product category]?" | Wikipedia | Neutral, comprehensive | Brand page is marketing-heavy |
| "Best [product] for [use case]" | G2 page | Aggregated reviews | Brand has no review aggregation |

### Step 4 — Design the PR strategy
This is strategic work that should involve the client's marketing team:

- **For media gaps**: Identify which publications are authoritative in this space. Pitch content or data stories that would earn links from them.
- **For aggregator gaps**: Encourage customers to leave reviews on G2/Trustpilot. Claim and optimise the brand's aggregator profiles.
- **For Wikipedia gaps**: Assess whether the brand meets Wikipedia's notability requirements. If yes, draft a neutral article or update an existing one.
- **For competitor content gaps**: Create the neutral, educational, genuinely useful piece of content that exists on the competitor's site but not on the brand's.

### Deliverable
A PR gap map table + a prioritised list of 3–5 PR/content actions with expected impact on AI citation share-of-voice. This must be approved by the client before execution.
