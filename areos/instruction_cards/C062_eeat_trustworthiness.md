# C062 — E-E-A-T & Trustworthiness Assessment

## What AREOS Does Automatically
- Checks for presence of author bio markup (`Person` schema with `name`, `jobTitle`, `sameAs`).
- Checks for `datePublished` and `dateModified` to indicate freshness.
- Flags missing editorial attribution.

## What You Must Do Manually

This check **requires you to read the content and apply domain judgment.** There is no substitute.

### Step 1 — Assess First-Hand Experience
Read the page's main content. Ask:
- Does the author demonstrate they have personally done or experienced what they're describing?
- Are there specific details, personal anecdotes, or original data that only someone with direct experience would know?
- OR is it generic, could-have-been-written-by-anyone content?

### Step 2 — Assess Expertise
- Is the author named?
- Can you verify their professional background (LinkedIn, company bio, published papers)?
- Do they demonstrate command of terminology without over-relying on jargon to signal expertise?
- Are claims sourced to credible external references (studies, official docs)?

### Step 3 — Assess Authoritativeness
- Is this a niche topic where the brand/author should be the authoritative source?
- Are other reputable sites linking to this page on this topic?
- Does the author's track record on this topic extend beyond this single page?

### Step 4 — Assess Trustworthiness
- Are the claims on the page verifiable?
- Are any statistics cited with a source, or stated as fact without backing?
- Are there any claims that feel overreaching, unverified, or potentially harmful (YMYL content)?

### Step 5 — Verdict
Rate E-E-A-T as: **Strong / Adequate / Weak / Absent**. Document your reasoning.

### Remediation
- **Experience:** Add a first-person case study, client story, or "lessons learned" section.
- **Expertise:** Add a visible author bio with credentials and link to LinkedIn.
- **Authoritativeness:** Build a topical authority cluster — link between related content. Pursue guest posts or press coverage.
- **Trustworthiness:** Add citations for every statistical claim. Add a review/update date.
